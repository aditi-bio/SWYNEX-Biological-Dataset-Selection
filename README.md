# SWYNEX Task 1 - Biological Dataset Selection

**Dataset Title:** β3-Adrenergic Signaling Preserves Postnatal Maturation of the Enteric Nervous System and Gut Microbiome During Neonatal Hyperoxia

**Source:** NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO)
**GEO Accession:** GSE344801
**Direct Link:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE344801
**Status:** Public on Sep 30, 2026
**Organism:** Rattus norvegicus (Rat)
**Experiment Type:** Other - 16S rRNA gene sequencing & Immunofluorescence
**Platform:** GPL25947
**Data Size:** 26 Samples, Format CSV/FASTA

**Summary:** Newborn rats were exposed to high oxygen (85% O2) to study how it affects gut nervous system and gut microbiome development.

**Research Question:** Can activation of β3-Adrenergic Receptor protect the gut from hyperoxia-induced damage in premature newborns?

**Why Important:** Helps in understanding premature infant health and oxygen therapy effects.
